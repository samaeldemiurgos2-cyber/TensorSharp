## Implemented models and validation scope

The families below are implemented. Their model cards identify the checkpoint, backend and device paths actually tested, and the remaining limits; support does not imply every quantization or device was validated. Choose the family-specific checkpoint and quantization that fit your hardware. More sizes and projector files: [Model Downloads](../MODEL_DOWNLOADS.md).
